US school-district panels, 2010-2019, from the Urban Institute Education Data Portal (public API; underlying sources:
NCES Common Core of Data school-district finance and enrollment, and ED EdFacts adjusted-cohort graduation rates 2011-2019).
Fetched with code/fetch_us_districts.py. Urban's missing-value codes are negative numbers. Graduation rates are reported as ranges; `grad_rate_midpt` is the midpoint.
finance.csv.gz     187,920 district-years (dollars)
enrollment.csv.gz  172,033 district-years (fall enrollment, all students)
grad_rates.csv.gz  107,221 district-years (all-students cohort)
