# National ODA targets: Norway, Sweden, Luxembourg (plus Netherlands, Denmark)

Method: pages fetched on 2026-11-22. Access note: oecd.org, one.oecd.org, oecd-ilibrary.org, regjeringen.no, devex.com all returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, and um.dk / government.nl returned 404 or an unusable page. Open copies of older OECD DAC peer reviews on cbd.int and the Swedish Riksdag site could be read. Statements below marked "search summary only" were NOT read on a page and are not in targets.csv.

## Norway - VERIFIED (historical)
- Source: OECD DAC Peer Review Norway 2013, https://www.cbd.int/financial/mainstream/norway-dac2013.pdf (PDF text read).
- Quote: "Allocating 1% of its GNI as ODA has been a firm commitment of Jens Stoltenberg's centre-left government, one enshrined in the Soria Moria Declaration (2005)".
- Quote: "Since 2009 Norway has spent about 1% of gross national income (GNI) on official development assistance (ODA) every year" and "announcing in the most recent DAC forward-spending survey that it is committed to providing 1% of GNI as ODA for the coming years."
- Quote: "there appears to be consensus among political parties that at a minimum Norway's ODA target should not be set below 0.7% of GNI" (after the Sept 2013 change of government).
- Not verified: current status (2019 review, 2022 mid-term review, 2023-25 figures). A search summary says the 2019 review calls 1% a continuing domestic commitment and the mid-term review warns of reputational risk from departing from it; the pages (OECD, regjeringen.no id2844317) returned 403. The earlier Norway row in targets.csv ("search-result summary only") remains unverified in that sense; a verified 2005 row was added.
- Unit is GNI (not GNP) in the text read; 1% of GNP was not found.

## Sweden - VERIFIED that the 1% goal existed in Dec 2022 and the government proposed to leave it; adoption year NOT verified
- Source: Riksdag committee report 2022/23:UU2 "Utgiftsområde 7 Internationellt bistånd" (decision date 2022-12-20 per the page's reference list), https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/betankande/utgiftsomrade-7-internationellt-bistand_ha01uu2/html/
- Quote (Centre Party motion): "avvisas regeringens förslag om att frångå det s.k. enprocentsmålet för biståndet."
- Quote (Social Democrat motion): "Socialdemokraterna står fast vid enprocentsmålet och avvisar regeringens förslag om att frångå detta för biståndsramen."
- Quote (committee): "regeringen anger att biståndsbudgeten ska uppgå till högst 56 miljarder kronor för 2023 och att samma nivå ska gälla för 2024 och 2025."
- So: the change took effect in the 2023 budget (decided Dec 2022, not "2023" as a decision year); replacement is a fixed ceiling of SEK 56 bn for 2023-2025. The text I read does not say when the 1% goal was first adopted, nor quote the government's own proposition wording (Prop. 2022/23:1 not read).
- Search summary only: OECD profile says 0.88% of GNI budgeted for 2023-25; Devex headline "UN, EU push back as Sweden drops 1 percent aid target" (403).

## Luxembourg - VERIFIED (historical)
- Source: OECD DAC Peer Review Luxembourg 2012, https://www.cbd.int/financial/mainstream/luxembourg-dac2012.pdf
- Quote: "official development assistance (ODA) has achieved and maintained since 2009 a remarkable level of 1% of gross national income (GNI), a target that Luxembourg had set for itself in 1999."
- Quote: "In its 2009-2014 programme, the government committed to maintaining the level of ODA at 1% of national income".
- Quote: "All political parties, with the exception of one minority party, want to keep the level of ODA at 1% of gross national income".
- Not verified: current status. Search summary only: the 2025 DAC peer review recommends continuing 1% of GNI and the 2023-2028 coalition keeps the objective (OECD pages 403).

## Netherlands - VERIFIED (0.7%, not 0.8%, as the budget norm from 2012; abandoned 2025 per secondary source)
- Source: OECD DAC Peer Review Netherlands 2011, https://www.cbd.int/financial/mainstream/netherlands-dac2011.pdf
- Quote: "It will cut ODA from 0.81% ODA/GNI in 2010 to 0.7% by 2012, with an interim step of 0.75% in 2011. From 2012 onwards the Dutch ODA budget will be 0.7% on average." 0.8% was the earlier basis ("maintaining its ODA at not less than 0.8% of national income through to 2015").
- Source (secondary): NL Times 2025-05-28, https://nltimes.nl/2025/05/28/dutch-govt-cut-nearly-eu5-bil-eu24-bil-development-aid-budget-2029. Quote: the government will tie aid to growth "in a new, simplified way that abandons the 0.7 percent standard."
- Not verified: primary Dutch government document for the 2025 change (government.nl fetch failed); OECD 2023 peer review and profile (403).

## Denmark - PARTIAL
- Source: OECD DAC Peer Review Denmark 2011, https://www.cbd.int/financial/mainstream/denmark-dac2011.pdf
- Quote: "the DAC encourages Denmark to return, as soon as possible, to its stated goal of 0.8% of aid to income ratio." Also: Denmark "has exceeded the UN target of providing 0.7% of national income ... for more than 30 years", and "The main opposition party, the Social Democrats, is committed to achieving 1% ODA/GNI."
- NOT verified: the current 0.7% GNI target (2017 Finance Act budget-balancing mechanism, 2025/2026 priorities papers). Search summaries only; um.dk PDF returned 404 and OECD pages 403. Not added to targets.csv.

## Answer to the question, as far as verified
Norway: yes, 1% of GNI, firm commitment from the 2005 Soria Moria Declaration (historical, to 2013 evidence). Luxembourg: yes, 1% of GNI, self-set 1999. Sweden: a "1% goal" existed and the government proposed to depart from it for the 2023 budget (Riksdag, Dec 2022), replaced with a SEK 56 bn ceiling. Netherlands: 0.7% budget norm from 2012, reportedly abandoned 2025. Denmark: 0.8% stated goal in 2011; present 0.7% unverified.
